# Japanese translation recordings

Generated locally with Open JTalk (pyopenjtalk 0.4.1), using the **Mei normal**
voice released by the MMDAgent Project Team, Nagoya Institute of Technology,
Department of Computer Science. Copyright © 2009–2013 Nagoya Institute of Technology.

Voice license: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Project: [MMDAgent](https://www.mmdagent.jp/).

The generated recordings are normalized to a PCM peak of −4 dBFS, resampled to
24 kHz mono, and encoded as 112 kbps MP3. English recordings are unchanged.
The index is `audio/ja_index.json`; filenames identify normalized translation text.
Generation and coverage checks: `for_chatgpt/generate_japanese_audio.py`.
